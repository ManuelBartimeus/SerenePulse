Products

Cover for the things you can’t afford to lose.

Eight lines of general insurance for individuals and businesses. Ask any branch which cover fits you.

  • Motor insurance

    Third Party Only, Third Party Fire & Theft, or Comprehensive cover, with an ECOWAS Brown Card for driving in the West African sub-region.

    Cover details for motor insurance

  • Fire & property insurance

    Protects buildings, contents, machinery and stock against fire, lightning and explosion, and can be extended to flood, storm, riot, burst pipes and more.

    Cover details for fire and property insurance

  • Marine insurance

    Keeps your imports and exports safe, including air cargo and other freight, under single or open covers. Marine hull cover is available for vessels.

    Cover details for marine insurance

  • Travel insurance

    Covers medical expenses, hospitalisation, repatriation to Ghana and lost or delayed baggage when you travel.

    Cover details for travel insurance

  • General accident insurance

    Money, goods-in-transit, burglary, fidelity guarantee, hoteliers, bankers’ indemnity and cyber crime policies for your business.

    Cover details for general accident insurance

  • Engineering insurance

    Contractors’ all risks, erection all risks and electronic equipment cover for construction projects, installations and machinery.

    Cover details for engineering insurance

  • Workmen’s compensation

    Covers an employer’s legal liability to employees for injury or disease at work, under the Workmen’s Compensation Law of 1987.

    Cover details for workmen’s compensation

  • Bonds

    Bid, performance and advance mobilisation bonds for contractors, and transit, warehouse, export and re-export bonds for importers.

    Cover details for bonds

Claims

A claim shouldn’t keep you waiting.

Our claim principle is simple: no outstanding claim should stay in our books more than one week after we receive your last document.

Small motor claims, such as dents, scratches or a broken windscreen, should be settled within a day of you providing the estimate.

Documents you need for each type of claim
Motor claim · partial loss What to bring
  • Completed and signed claim form
  • Repair estimate from a garage
  • Photographs of the damaged vehicle
  • Towage receipt, if you needed one
  • Driver’s licence

Fire, accident and marine claims have their own lists.

About us

The new face of insurance.

Serene Insurance Company Limited was incorporated on 21 July 2016 and is a member of the First Sky Group. We received our commencement certificate on 4 April 2018 and started full operations in September 2018.

We are licensed by the National Insurance Commission to carry out general (non-life) insurance business in Ghana, and we are capitalised above the Commission’s regulatory minimum. A professional Board of Directors oversees our operations.

“Our ambition is to be the leading model for delivering value all the time in the insurance industry.”
